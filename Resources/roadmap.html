Rule of thumb for breaking camp

You don't need mastery to move to the next phase — you need to be able to use what this camp taught you without constantly looking things up. If you're still confused on the fundamentals, stay put. If you're just slow but you understand it, move on and let practice sharpen you. Math (Camp 0) is the one exception — it runs alongside everything else rather than gating it.
